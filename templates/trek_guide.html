Essential 3-Layer Packing System

  • Base Layer: 2x Merino wool / thermal synthetic moisture-wicking tops & bottoms.
  • Insulation Layer: 1x Heavy Fleece jacket + 1x Down feather jacket (-10°C rated).
  • Outer Shell: Waterproof Gore-Tex hooded jacket & windproof trekking pants.
  • Footwear: High-ankle waterproof trekking shoes (Vibram sole) + 4x wool socks.
  • Gear: 50–60L Backpack with rain cover, UV400 Polarized sunglasses, Headlamp with extra batteries.
